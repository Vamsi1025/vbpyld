About The Dialogue

A two-day conclave for young leaders, professionals, and changemakers.

The Viksit Bharat Young Political Leaders' Dialogue brings together eminent political leaders, policymakers, entrepreneurs, and change makers to discuss India's democratic journey, governance reforms, leadership values, and the roadmap towards Viksit Bharat @2047, with a special focus on Andhra Pradesh's contribution.
